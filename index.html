A bit about me

I'm Aditya Bansal, a CS undergrad at BITS Pilani. I'm broadly interested in AI safety: making AI systems safer, in practice, for the people who use them. Through research projects and internships, I've assessed the trustworthiness of AI systems, run black-box audits of deployed products, and carried out backdoor attacks on LLMs to understand red-teaming.
